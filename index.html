Our Story

Crafting sweetness in the heart of Kathmandu since 2019

Kathmandu Cake House was born from a simple belief: that every celebration deserves something truly extraordinary.

Founded in 2019 in the heart of Nepal's capital, we set out to redefine what a bakery could be. Drawing inspiration from European patisseries and the rich flavors of our homeland, we created a space where luxury meets warmth, where every cake tells a story.

Our team of passionate cake artists combines traditional techniques with modern innovation, using only the finest ingredients sourced from around the world and fresh local produce. From Belgian chocolate to Madagascar vanilla, every element is chosen with care.

Today, Kathmandu Cake House has become synonymous with celebration in the Kathmandu Valley. Whether it's a grand wedding, an intimate anniversary, or a simple gesture of love, we're honored to be part of your most precious moments.
